03 Partner Practice

Say it out loud before it's real

Describe your partner. Then practice what you want to say with an AI that plays them, including the pushback you'd expect. Afterwards you get a short, honest debrief.

  • An AI that plays your partner, based on the personality you describe and what it learned from your chats
  • A clear debrief every time: what went well, what to watch for, what to try next
  • Switch roles to feel how your own words might sound to them
  • Completely private. It never contacts your real partner

FAQ

Still have questions?

What is RelationshipAI?

It's a private AI relationship coach. You can talk things through in Coach Chat, check a message before you send it with Message check, and practice a hard conversation in Partner Practice. It's built on methods real therapists use, like the Gottman Method, attachment theory and Nonviolent Communication.

Is RelationshipAI free?

Yes. There is a Free plan that never expires: 8 Coach Chat messages a day, 3 Message checks, one Partner Practice rehearsal (up to 8 messages) with a debrief, the calming guide for after a fight, a Playbook, and a daily check-in. Upgrade to Pro or Premium only if you want more room. No card is needed to start.

What is Message check?

You paste a message you're thinking of sending, and optionally the message you're replying to. In a few seconds it tells you how the message may land (for example as blame or pressure) and gives you two or three better versions to choose from. The text you paste isn't stored. We only keep a counter, so we can apply your plan's limit.

What is Coach Chat?

Coach Chat is the main conversation with your AI coach. You describe what's going on, and it asks follow-up questions, remembers your story across chats, notices patterns, and helps you work out next steps and the words to use.

What is Partner Practice? Is it talking to my real partner?

No, it never contacts your real partner. You describe their personality, and the AI plays them as a private rehearsal, including realistic pushback. After each rehearsal you get a short debrief: what went well, what to watch for, and one thing to try next. Nothing you practice is shared with anyone.

What is After a fight?

A short guide for the first hour after an argument. It helps you calm down first, then (on Pro and Premium) writes a short message you can send to reach out. Premium messages are tailored to your partner and you get a gentle check-in the next day.

What is the Playbook?

When the coach suggests something small to try, such as a sentence to say or a question to ask, you can tap "I'll try it". A couple of days later we ask how it went. Over time you get a private list of what worked for you and what didn't, so the advice stops being generic. Only you can see it.

Can I share things with my partner?

Only if you choose to. You pick specific things, like one chat or a summary, and send your partner a link. They don't need an account. Everything else stays private to you.

Is RelationshipAI a replacement for therapy?

No. It's a self-improvement tool for support between therapy sessions, or when therapy isn't easy to get. For serious problems, abuse, or a crisis, we always recommend contacting a professional.

Does it work for any kind of relationship?

Yes. Dating, long-term, married, long-distance, or non-monogamous. The coaching adapts to what you tell it about your situation instead of following a fixed script.

What if my partner doesn't want to use it?

That's fine. It works even if you're the only one using it. Most change in a relationship starts with one person showing up differently, and Partner Practice lets you prepare for real conversations on your own.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Only you can see your conversations when you're logged in, and we never share them with third parties. Texts you paste into Message check and After a fight are not stored.

What happens when I reach a Free plan limit?

Coach Chat's 8 daily messages reset every day, and nothing is lost. Partner Practice is one rehearsal of up to 8 messages with a debrief (it doesn't use your daily Coach messages), Message check gives 3 tries, and you can add 3 screenshots or files in total. You can still read everything you already have. To keep going, upgrade to Pro.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro takes the Free limits away: up to 1,000 AI messages a month with no daily limit, unlimited Partner Practice on our stronger AI, up to 5 partner profiles, unlimited files, Message check (10 a day), a personal After a fight message (3 a month), weekly focus, ideas and insights, and 30 Advanced Coach Chat messages a month. Premium includes everything in Pro with up to 1,500 AI messages a month, and adds the deeper tools: Advanced AI in Coach Chat, a monthly review, a short Talk plan before each rehearsal, Message check and After a fight with no limit and tailored to you, your year so far, hard mode with a readiness score, a Therapist pack, Playbook analysis, unlimited partner profiles, and priority support.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no hidden conditions. Cancel or switch plans anytime from your account page and you keep access until the end of your billing period. Every paid plan, monthly or yearly, has a 7-day money-back guarantee.

What AI does it use?

Free uses a fast, efficient OpenAI model with a prompt made for relationship coaching. Paid plans include a monthly amount of messages on our most advanced model: 150 Partner Practice messages and 30 Coach Chat messages on Pro, and 300 messages on Premium (Coach Chat, Message check and After a fight). After that we switch you to the fast model until the 1st, so you're never blocked. In every chat you can also choose Fast or Advanced yourself, for example to save your Advanced messages for the conversations that matter most.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. RelationshipAI runs in your browser and works well on a phone in the meantime.
