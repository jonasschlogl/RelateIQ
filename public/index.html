Everything is built around one goal: a better conversation

Coach Chat helps you understand what's going on and what to say. Partner Practice lets you say it out loud first.

A coach that already knows your story

Describe what's happening the way you'd tell a friend. Coach Chat remembers your partner, your history and last week's fight, so you never start from scratch, and gives you specific next steps instead of vague reassurance.

02 Partner Practice

Rehearse the hard conversation before it's real

Describe your partner, then practice what you want to say with an AI that stays in character, including the realistic friction. When you're done, you get a short, honest debrief aimed at the real conversation still ahead.

  • An AI that plays your partner based on the personality you describe, and what it's learned from your chats
  • A clear debrief after each rehearsal: what went well, what to watch for, one thing to try differently
  • Switch roles to hear how your own words might land on the receiving end
  • Completely private. It's never in contact with your real partner and nothing is shared with them

FAQ

Still have questions?

What is the Playbook?

When the Coach gives you something small to try, such as a sentence to say or a question to ask, you can tap "I'll try it". A couple of days later we ask how it went. Over time you get a private list of what worked for you and what didn't, so advice stops being generic. It is only visible to you.

Is RelationshipAI free?

Yes, there is a Free plan that never expires: 8 Coach Chat messages a day, one Partner Practice rehearsal (up to 8 messages) with a debrief, and a daily check-in. Upgrade to Pro or Premium only if you want no daily limit, unlimited Partner Practice and more room to work with. No card is needed to start.

Is RelationshipAI a replacement for therapy?

No. RelationshipAI is a self-improvement tool for immediate support between therapy sessions, or when therapy isn't accessible. For serious issues, abuse, or a crisis situation, we always recommend seeking a professional.

What is Coach Chat?

Coach Chat is the main conversation with your AI relationship coach. You describe what's going on, and it asks follow-up questions, remembers your story across conversations, notices recurring patterns, and helps you work out concrete next steps and the words to use.

What is Partner Practice, and is it actually talking to my partner?

No, it never talks to your real partner. You describe your partner's personality and the AI plays them as a private rehearsal space, including realistic friction. After each rehearsal you get a short debrief: what went well, what to watch for, and one thing to try differently. Nothing you practice is shared with anyone.

Does RelationshipAI work for any kind of relationship?

Yes. Dating, long-term, married, long-distance, or non-monogamous relationships. The coaching adapts to what you tell it about your specific situation rather than following a fixed script.

What if my partner doesn't want to use it?

That's completely fine. RelationshipAI works even if you're the only one using it. Most change in a relationship starts with how one person shows up differently, and Partner Practice exists so you can prepare for real conversations solo.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Only you can see your conversations once you're logged into your account, and we never share them with third parties.

What happens when I hit a Free plan limit?

Coach Chat's 8 daily messages reset every day. You'll get a friendly heads-up and can pick back up tomorrow, nothing is lost. Partner Practice is one rehearsal of up to 8 messages with a debrief (it doesn't use your daily Coach messages), and you can attach 3 screenshots or files in total. After that, upgrade to Pro for unlimited attachments.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro removes the Free-plan limits: no daily message limit (fair use applies), unlimited Partner Practice rehearsals on our stronger AI model with anyone you want to practice with, up to 5 partner profiles, unlimited screenshots and files, weekly insights, a Playbook where you can try several things at once with your full history, Message check (10 a day), a personal After a fight message (3 a month), and a weekly focus with weekly ideas. Premium includes everything in Pro and adds the deeper tools: Coach Chat on our most advanced AI model, a monthly review of how things are going, a short plan before each rehearsal (opening line, what to avoid, how they might react), unlimited Message check and After a fight messages tailored to them (with a check-in the next day), a fuller weekly focus with five tailored ideas, a year-so-far recap, Practice hard mode with a readiness score that tracks your progress, a Therapist pack that puts your whole story on one page, a Playbook analysis, unlimited partner profiles, priority support and early access to new features.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No commitment, no hidden conditions. Cancel or switch plans anytime from your account page and you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period. Every paid plan, monthly or yearly, includes a 7-day money-back guarantee.

What AI model does it use?

Free runs on a fast, efficient OpenAI model with a prompt tuned specifically for relationship coaching. Pro upgrades Coach Chat and Partner Practice to a smarter model for more nuanced answers, and Premium runs on our most advanced model. If you use a lot of the top-tier messages in a month, we gracefully switch you to the next model down instead of ever blocking you.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. RelationshipAI runs entirely in your browser and works well on mobile browsers in the meantime.
