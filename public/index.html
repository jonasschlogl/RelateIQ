FAQ

Still have questions?

Is RelateIQ a replacement for therapy?

No. RelateIQ is a self-improvement tool for immediate support between therapy sessions, or when therapy isn't accessible. For serious issues, abuse, or a crisis situation, we always recommend seeking a professional.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Only you can see your conversations once you're logged into your account, and we never share them with third parties.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no commitment, no hidden conditions. Cancel or switch plans anytime from your account page, and you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

What happens when I hit the Free plan's daily limit?

You'll get a friendly heads-up in the chat and can pick back up the next day — nothing is lost, and your full conversation history stays exactly as it was. Upgrading to Pro removes the daily limit entirely.

What's actually different between Pro and Premium?

Pro removes the daily AI message limit and raises your partner profile limit to 5. Premium adds unlimited partner profiles, priority support, and early access to new modes as they ship.

What AI model does it use?

RelateIQ runs on OpenAI's GPT-4o mini, with a prompt tuned specifically for relationship coaching.

Is Partner Practice actually talking to my partner?

No. You describe your partner's personality and the AI roleplays as them, purely as a rehearsal space for you — it's never in contact with your real partner and nothing you practice is shared with them.

Do I have to take the quiz or do the daily check-in?

No — both are entirely optional. You can use RelateIQ for Coach Chat only and never touch them, or skip either one at any time with no impact on the rest of the app.
