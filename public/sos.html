After a fight

Take a breath first

A minute to settle, then a few words to reach out with. Nothing here is sent to anyone.

1. Settle your body

Ready?

2. For the next hour

  • Don't keep arguing over text. It rarely goes well in writing.
  • Drink some water and move a little: walk, stairs, step outside.
  • Don't decide anything big about the relationship right now.
  • Wait to send anything until your heart rate has dropped.

If you don't feel safe, contact your local emergency number or someone you trust right away.
