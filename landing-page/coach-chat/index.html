Asks before it advises

One to three specific questions first, so the advice fits your situation and not a generic couple.

Names the pattern

Spots cycles like pursue and withdraw, gives them a plain name and reuses it, so you can see it next time.

Remembers you and your partner

Keeps context across chats and builds a picture of your partner, so you never start from zero.

Honest, not a yes-machine

It does not just agree. It considers what your partner would say, and kindly names your share.

Draws on real couples methods

Gottman, Emotionally Focused Therapy, CBT, nonviolent communication, Imago and more, blended to fit you.

Plans, not just comfort

Concrete pieces of work: a weekly relationship talk, a time-out agreement, a listening exercise, with when and how.

Try-this cards that follow up

Save a suggestion as an experiment. Two days later it asks how it went and adjusts the next step.

Reads screenshots, hears your voice

Add photos or screenshots of the real conversation, or dictate. It replies in the language you write in.

A summary for your therapist

Turn a chat into a one-page summary to print or bring to your own therapist.
