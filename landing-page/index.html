Quick answers.

What is RelationshipAI?

A private AI relationship coach. Talk things through, check a message before you send it, and practice a hard conversation. It draws on methods used in real couples therapy, such as the Gottman Method, attachment theory and Nonviolent Communication. It is not affiliated with the Gottman Institute or any of these organizations.

When does it open?

Soon. Leave your email and we'll write to you once, the day it opens.

Is it a replacement for therapy?

No. It's a self-improvement tool for support between sessions, or when therapy isn't easy to get. For serious problems, abuse, or a crisis, we always recommend contacting a professional.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Only you can see them when you're logged in. We never sell them or use them for ads. To write replies they are processed by our AI provider (OpenAI), which doesn't train its models on them under its API terms. Texts you paste into Message check and After a fight are not stored.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract. Cancel or switch plans from your account page and keep access until the end of your billing period.
