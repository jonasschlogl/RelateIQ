FAQ

Still have questions?

What is RelationshipAI?

A private AI relationship coach: talk things through, check a message before you send it, and rehearse hard conversations. It draws on methods like the Gottman Method, attachment theory and Nonviolent Communication, but is not affiliated with any of these organizations.

When does RelationshipAI open?

Soon. If you leave your email, we'll write to you once, the day it opens. Clicks on “Get early access” are counted anonymously.

What is Partner Practice? Is it talking to my real partner?

No, it never contacts your real partner. The AI plays them as a private rehearsal, then gives you a short debrief.

Can I share things with my partner?

Only if you choose to. You send a link to specific things, and they don't need an account.

Is RelationshipAI a replacement for therapy?

No. It's a self-improvement tool for between sessions, or when therapy isn't easy to get. For serious problems, abuse or a crisis, please contact a professional.

Does it work for any kind of relationship?

Yes. Dating, long-term, married, long-distance or non-monogamous.

What if my partner doesn't want to use it?

That's fine. It works with just you, and Partner Practice lets you prepare on your own.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Only you can see them. We never sell them or use them for ads. They're processed by our AI provider (OpenAI) to write replies, and it doesn't train on them under its API terms. Texts pasted into Message check and After a fight are not stored.

What happens when I reach a Free plan limit?

Coach Chat's 8 daily messages reset every day. You can still read everything you have. To keep going, upgrade to Pro.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro removes the Free limits: up to 1,000 AI messages a month, unlimited Partner Practice, up to 5 partner profiles and more. Premium adds up to 1,500 messages, Advanced AI in Coach Chat, monthly review, Talk plan, hard mode, Therapist pack and priority support. See the full comparison above.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel or switch plans from your account page and keep access until the end of your billing period. Payments are not refundable, except where the law gives you a right to a refund.

What AI does it use?

Free uses a fast OpenAI model tuned for relationship coaching. Paid plans add a monthly amount of messages on our most advanced model, and you can pick Fast or Advanced in each chat.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. RelationshipAI runs in your browser and works well on a phone in the meantime.
