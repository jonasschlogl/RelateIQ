FAQ

Good questions.

Does it talk to my real partner?

No, never. The AI plays your partner as a private rehearsal. Nothing is sent to anyone, and your partner never sees it.

How does it know what my partner is like?

From the profile you create: their name, personality and what’s going on. You can also paste a few of their real messages, and it learns their patterns from your Coach Chat conversations.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes one Partner Practice rehearsal of up to 8 messages, with a debrief. No card needed.

Is it a replacement for therapy?

No. It’s a self-improvement tool for preparing hard conversations. For serious problems, abuse or a crisis, please contact a professional.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Only you can see them. We never sell them or use them for ads. They’re processed by our AI provider to write replies, and it doesn’t train on them under its API terms.
