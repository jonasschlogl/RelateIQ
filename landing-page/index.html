FAQ

Still have questions?

What is RelationshipAI?

It's a private AI relationship coach. You can talk things through in Coach Chat, check a message before you send it with Message check, and practice a hard conversation in Partner Practice. It draws on methods used in real couples therapy, such as the Gottman Method, attachment theory and Nonviolent Communication. RelationshipAI is not affiliated with the Gottman Institute or any of these organizations.

When does RelationshipAI open?

Soon. We're putting the finishing touches on it and checking how many people are interested before we open the doors. Clicking any "Get early access" button is counted, anonymously, and helps us decide how quickly to open. If you leave your email, we'll write to you once, the day it opens.

What is Partner Practice? Is it talking to my real partner?

No, it never contacts your real partner. You describe their personality, and the AI plays them as a private rehearsal, including realistic pushback. After each rehearsal you get a short debrief: what went well, what to watch for, and one thing to try next. Nothing you practice is shared with anyone.

What is After a fight?

A short guide for the first hour after an argument. It helps you calm down first, then (on Pro and Premium) writes a short message you can send to reach out. Premium messages are tailored to your partner and you get a gentle check-in the next day.

What is the Playbook?

When the coach suggests something small to try, such as a sentence to say or a question to ask, you can tap "I'll try it". A couple of days later we ask how it went. Over time you get a private list of what worked for you and what didn't, so the advice stops being generic. Only you can see it.

Can I share things with my partner?

Only if you choose to. You pick specific things, like one chat or a summary, and send your partner a link. They don't need an account. Everything else stays private to you.

Is RelationshipAI a replacement for therapy?

No. It's a self-improvement tool for support between therapy sessions, or when therapy isn't easy to get. For serious problems, abuse, or a crisis, we always recommend contacting a professional.

Does it work for any kind of relationship?

Yes. Dating, long-term, married, long-distance, or non-monogamous. The coaching adapts to what you tell it about your situation instead of following a fixed script.

What if my partner doesn't want to use it?

That's fine. It works even if you're the only one using it. Most change in a relationship starts with one person showing up differently, and Partner Practice lets you prepare for real conversations on your own.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Only you can see your conversations when you're logged in. We never sell them or use them for ads. To write your replies they are processed by our AI provider (OpenAI), which doesn't train its models on them under its API terms. Texts you paste into Message check and After a fight are not stored.

What happens when I reach a Free plan limit?

Coach Chat's 8 daily messages reset every day, and nothing is lost. Partner Practice is one rehearsal of up to 8 messages with a debrief (it doesn't use your daily Coach messages), Message check gives 3 tries, and you can add 3 screenshots or files in total. You can still read everything you already have. To keep going, upgrade to Pro.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro takes the Free limits away: up to 1,000 AI messages a month with no daily limit, unlimited Partner Practice on our stronger AI, up to 5 partner profiles, unlimited files, Message check (10 a day), a personal After a fight message (3 a month), weekly focus, ideas and insights, and 80 Advanced Coach Chat messages a month. Premium includes everything in Pro with up to 1,500 AI messages a month, and adds the deeper tools: Advanced AI in Coach Chat, a monthly review, a short Talk plan before each rehearsal, Message check and After a fight with no limit and tailored to you, your year so far, hard mode with a readiness score, a Therapist pack, Playbook analysis, unlimited partner profiles, and priority support.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no hidden conditions. Cancel or switch plans anytime from your account page and you keep access until the end of your billing period. Payments are not refundable, except where the law gives you a right to a refund.

What AI does it use?

Free uses a fast, efficient OpenAI model with a prompt made for relationship coaching. Paid plans include a monthly amount of messages on our most advanced model: 200 Partner Practice messages and 80 Coach Chat messages on Pro, and 500 messages on Premium (Coach Chat, Partner Practice, Message check and After a fight). After that we switch you to the fast model until the 1st, so you're never blocked. In every chat you can also choose Fast or Advanced yourself, for example to save your Advanced messages for the conversations that matter most.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. RelationshipAI runs in your browser and works well on a phone in the meantime.
