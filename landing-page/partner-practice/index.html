Plays your partner, in character

Create a profile with their name, personality and the context. The AI answers as they would, so the pushback feels real.

Learns who they are from your chats

It builds a picture of your partner from your past Coach Chat conversations: traits, communication patterns, how they tend to react.

Learns from their real words

Paste messages they have actually sent. It picks up how they phrase things and rehearses in that voice.

A debrief after every rehearsal

What went well, what to watch for, and one concrete tip for the real conversation.

A readiness score, 1 to 10

One honest number for how ready you seem, so you know when to keep practising and when you are set.

A talk plan before you startPremium

An opening line, what to avoid and how to respond if it heats up, linked to your rehearsal.

Hard modePremium
