What makes an apology work, according to research

Researchers at Ohio State University, led by Roy Lewicki, tested six parts of an apology with 755 people: expressing regret, explaining what went wrong, acknowledging responsibility, declaring repentance, offering to repair, and asking for forgiveness.

The most important part by far was acknowledging responsibility: saying clearly that it was your fault. The second was an offer of repair. The least important was asking for forgiveness. In other words, "please forgive me" matters less than "I did this, and here is what I'll do".

Apology texts that backfire, and better versions

BeforeI'm sorry you feel that way.

AfterI'm sorry I said that. It was unfair, and I can see why it hurt.

BeforeSorry, but you overreacted too.

AfterI'm sorry I raised my voice. We can talk about the rest later, but this part was on me.

BeforeI said sorry, what else do you want?

AfterI know one sorry doesn't fix it. What would help you feel better about this?

BeforeSorry if I upset you.

AfterI'm sorry I canceled last minute. Can I make it up to you on Saturday?

Notice what changes: no "but", no "if", no "you feel". The better version names the thing you did and, where it fits, offers something concrete.

When a text is the right place to say sorry

A text works well for small things, or as a first step when the other person needs space. For something bigger, use the text to open the door, then apologize properly in person: "I owe you a real apology. Can we talk tonight?" Quick answers

How do I apologize over text to my girlfriend or boyfriend?

Keep it short, name what you did, take responsibility without "but", and offer something concrete, like talking tonight. Avoid long explanations in the first message. They read as excuses.

Should I apologize over text or in person?

For small things, a text is fine. For something that really hurt, use a text to say you are sorry and ask for a time to talk, then apologize in person.
